---
name: grill-me
description: A relentless interview to sharpen a plan or design, with nothing written down. User-invoked.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Load the `grilling` skill and run it on the plan, decision or idea in front of
you. Write nothing to the repository: use this when there is no working
directory, or when the outcome should stay in the conversation. Inside a
repository, `grill-with-docs` runs the same interview and leaves a record.
